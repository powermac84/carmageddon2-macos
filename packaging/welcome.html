Carmageddon II for Apple Silicon

A native Mac launcher and compatibility runtime for your own copy of Carmageddon II.

Original game data is not included. After installation, open the app and import a supported GOG setup EXE, an installed PC game folder, or an existing Mac wrapper containing the PC version. You can also copy your files into the app's Import folder later.

This installer installs only the app. It does not replace your older Carmageddon II.app or delete your saved games. Run one Carmageddon II app at a time.

Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. Classic Mac-only PowerPC game media is not supported by this release. This community preview is not affiliated with the original publisher.
